Clinical Pathophysiology

Understanding the Tissue Root Cause

Ingrown hairs occur when curved hair shafts pierce the follicular wall or curl back into the epidermis, sparking chronic foreign-body inflammation, pustules, and hyperpigmentation. Shaving, waxing, and tweezing perpetuate this inflammatory cycle.

Expected Clinical Trajectory

80% to 90%+ permanent hair reduction after a completed clinical series of 6 sessions, with complete cessation of ingrown bumps and razor rash.

GOLD STANDARD TECHNOLOGY

Dual-Wavelength Medical Laser Epilation

Selectively targets melanin within the hair bulb at the root, delivering thermal energy that permanently disables the follicular germ center while preserving surface skin.
