The Historic H.H. Richardson Sanctuary

Designed in the late 19th century by master architect Henry Hobson Richardson (famed originator of the Richardsonian Romanesque style), our Staten Island headquarters is an officially designated New York City landmark.

Located at 45 McClean Avenue, this grand stone house offers patients total discretion and serenity away from commercial high-rises. Surrounded by mature trees and Romanesque arches, guests experience physician treatments in an atmosphere of enduring peace.
