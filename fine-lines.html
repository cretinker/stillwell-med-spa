Clinical Pathophysiology

Understanding the Tissue Root Cause

As natural biological aging progresses past our late 20s, fibroblast activity slows and collagen breaks down at approximately 1% annually. Dynamic facial muscles create persistent surface creases, while fat pads descend. Our protocol tackles both levels: relaxing repetitive contraction and stimulating dense collagen remodeling at 1.5mm depths.

Expected Clinical Trajectory

Most patients see progressive skin tightening at 6 to 12 weeks, with sustained structural volume restoration lasting up to 2 full years.

GOLD STANDARD TECHNOLOGY

Sofwave Parallel Ultrasound + Sculptra PLLA

Sofwave delivers FDA-cleared parallel ultrasound energy to shrink and tighten mid-dermis, while Sculptra microparticles trigger autologous Type 1 collagen scaffolding over 24 months.
