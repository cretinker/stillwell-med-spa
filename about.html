Historic Sanctuary

The Landmark H.H. Richardson House

Designed in the late 19th century by master architect Henry Hobson Richardson (famed originator of the Richardsonian Romanesque style), our Staten Island headquarters is an officially designated New York City landmark.

Located at 45 McClean Avenue, this historic home offers our patients total privacy and serenity. Surrounded by tranquil grounds and majestic architectural arches, guests experience clinical rejuvenation in an atmosphere of enduring elegance.
