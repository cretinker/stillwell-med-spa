Clinical Pathophysiology

Understanding the Tissue Root Cause

Severe inflammatory acne causes deep dermal collagen destruction, leaving behind rigid fibrotic tethering that manifests as icepick, boxcar, or rolling scars. Topical skincare cannot penetrate deeply enough to remodel these scarred subterranean collagen bundles.

Expected Clinical Trajectory

Gradual smoothing of indented scars and structural skin elevation over 3 to 6 months as newly synthesized collagen fills scarred valleys.

GOLD STANDARD TECHNOLOGY

Tetra Pro CO2 vaporizes micro-columns of damaged tissue with clean Deka pulses, while Genius RF microneedles deliver thermal energy up to 3.5mm deep to stimulate fresh, supple elastin.
