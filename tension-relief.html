Clinical Pathophysiology

Understanding the Tissue Root Cause

Debilitating stress, posture strain, or spinal misalignments lead to hyper-irritable taut bands in skeletal muscle fibers known as trigger points. These knots generate chronic referred pain, occipital tension headaches, and severe mobility restriction.

Expected Clinical Trajectory

Immediate relaxation of locked muscular fibers, dramatic reduction in headache frequency, and restored cervical and lumbar range of motion.
